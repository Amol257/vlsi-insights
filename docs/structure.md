**VLSI Insights — Full Website Design**

Dark-themed website for a semiconductor training and services company. The aesthetic references integrated circuit layouts — precise, technical, quietly high-end. Near-black backgrounds, electric teal accents, Space Grotesk headings, Inter body text. No drop shadows, no decorative gradients, no soft rounded SaaS cards. Border color changes and subtle lifts are the only hover signals.

**Global palette:** Background #0D1117, card surface #161E2A, borders #1E2A3A, primary accent teal #00C9A7, CTA amber #D4870A, body text #94A3B8, headings #E8EAED.

**SHARED DESIGN RULES (apply to every page)**

No drop shadows on any card or element. No decorative gradients used as section backgrounds. No all-caps section body text. No centered body content — all text blocks are left-aligned. No middle-dot separators, no em dashes, no arrow suffixes on buttons. Teal is used at full opacity only for: icons, active states, borders on hover, CTA text, and the primary button. Everything else stays in the dark grey palette. The circuit trace SVG motif appears only in the hero and footer per page — not repeated between sections.
