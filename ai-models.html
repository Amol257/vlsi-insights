Edge AI & Model Optimization Services

Tailored machine learning services designed specifically for constrained embedded hardware, edge accelerators, and silicon prototyping.

TinyML & Microcontroller Inference

Deploying lightweight neural nets onto resource-constrained MCUs with under 256KB RAM using TensorFlow Lite Micro, Edge Impulse, and CMSIS-NN kernels.

  • ARM Cortex-M0+/M4/M7/M33 optimization
  • Minimal SRAM footprint buffering
  • Deterministic latency execution cycles

Model Quantization & Compression

Converting heavy FP32 weights into post-training INT8 or Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) structures with weight pruning and structural sparsity.

  • Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) calibration
  • Knowledge distillation for edge networks
  • Zero-loss accuracy retention pipelines

Embedded Computer Vision

Real-time visual detection pipelines tailored for edge cameras, industrial defect inspection, presence detection, and gesture interfaces on edge SoCs.

  • YOLO-nano & MobileNet architectures
  • Low-latency frame pipelining & DMA transfer
  • Automated industrial anomaly detection

FPGA Hardware AI Accelerators

Synthesizable systolic array MAC engines and custom Verilog/SystemVerilog convolution units on AMD Xilinx and Intel FPGA platforms.

  • Vivado HLS & Vitis AI toolchain mapping
  • High-throughput streaming AXI datapaths
  • Custom quantization bit-width architectures

Acoustic & Keyword Spotting (KWS)

Always-on acoustic sensing, voice wake-word triggers, and vibration anomaly classification for predictive maintenance in industrial automation.

  • MFCC feature extraction in hardware
  • Low standby power voice triggers
  • Industrial motor vibration diagnostics

Edge MLOps & Profiling

End-to-end continuous benchmarking, hardware-in-the-loop validation, automated regression testing, and over-the-air model updating infrastructure.

  • Cycle-accurate power & latency profiling
  • Automated conversion to ONNX / TFLite
  • Secure OTA model weights distribution

Deployment Lifecycle

The Edge AI Silicon Workflow

How we transition machine learning models from floating-point training to low-power edge silicon execution.

Stage 01
Requirements
Target hardware audit, RAM/Flash budgets, frame rate, and power targets.
Stage 02
Architecture
Selection of compact topologies (MobileNet, SqueezeNet, custom 1D/2D CNNs).
Stage 03
Quantization
INT8 / FP16 calibration with representative dataset profiling.
Stage 04
Target Compiler
Compiling to TFLite Micro, CMSIS-NN, or FPGA bitstreams.
Stage 05
Hardware Profile
Benchmarking cycle times, cache misses, and current draw on real boards.
Stage 06
Deployment
Firmware driver integration, production flashing, and test bench sign-off.
