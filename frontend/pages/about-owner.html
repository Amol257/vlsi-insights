Vision

In His Own Words

"Empowering the Next Generation of VLSI Enthusiasts every electronics graduate deserves a real shot at a career in chip design."

I started VLSI Insights because I lived the frustration myself. Fresh out of engineering college, I had a degree but no practical skills that companies actually look for. There was a huge gap between what universities teach and what the semiconductor industry needs and almost nobody was bridging it.

After working at companies like Agnisys and NXP Semiconductors and getting an M.Tech with Honours, I realised I could use everything I learned on the job to help the next batch of engineers skip the years of confusion I went through. Since founding VLSI Insights in 2023, we have trained over 500 students and placed them in technology companies across India and abroad.

Professional Journey

2021 - 2022 • ADVANCED ENGINEERING

Design Verification Engineer, Agnisys

Worked at a leading chip automation company, specialising in verifying that chip designs behave correctly before they go into production. Also completed an M.Tech with Honours from AKTU (Ajay Kumar Garg Engineering College) alongside this role.

2022 - 2023 • SEMICONDUCTOR LEADERSHIP

Engineer, NXP Semiconductors & Semi Designs

Joined NXP Semiconductors one of the world's largest chip makers working on complex digital chip projects. Also published two research papers in IEEE, the world's leading engineering journal.

2023 - PRESENT • FOUNDER

CEO & Founder, VLSI Insights

Founded VLSI Insights to close the gap between college education and industry expectations. Has since trained over 500 students and placed them in top technology companies, partnering with 50+ MNCs along the way.
