Your Engineering Journey

Campus to semiconductor floor

A structured, six-stage framework designed to transform theoretical knowledge into verified silicon engineering capability.

01

Orientation & Foundations

Establish solid ground in Boolean synthesis, Linux terminal workflows, and basic digital timing concepts.

02

RTL & Architecture Labs

Design synthesizable state machines, memory controllers, and arithmetic datapaths using clean Verilog HDL.

03

EDA Simulation & Debug

Simulate designs on industry tools (QuestaSim / VCS), analyze waveforms, and isolate subtle timing races.

04

Advanced Protocol Verification

Build constrained random UVM environments, integrate SVA assertions, and verify AMBA bus protocols.

05

Capstone Silicon Project

Architect and verify a comprehensive SoC subsystem (RISC-V / DMA controller) under senior engineering review.

06

Placement & Mock Interviews

Rigorous 1-on-1 technical interview prep, ATS resume audit, and direct referrals to hiring semiconductor MNCs.
